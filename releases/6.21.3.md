# 6.21.3

Fixes the Prism Break planning stall, where Gabriel's planning commitment became Blocked before he could review a completed Story.

- A planning responsibility that was already resolved against the current approved brief stays resolved. A later review that reports the same gap again, with no change to the brief, no longer asks the platform to reopen it. A changed approved brief is still a new basis and can raise the gap again.
- Issue tracking runs beside planning. If an issue update is rejected or fails, Gabriel records the reason on his timeline, keeps existing holds on the affected work, and continues to review and publish independent work. The update is retried on the next planning pass.
- Staffing and resource readiness no longer cause the ownership review to be judged again. A toolchain becoming Ready does not produce a second verdict on an unchanged roadmap.
- Gabriel's publish-or-revise decision on a returned Story is reused while the draft, approved scope, recorded direction, findings, acceptance and holds on that Story are unchanged. A decision to wait is always judged again against current facts.
- When the next step is Gabriel's own, such as requesting a revision he just decided on, he continues after one minute. The 15-minute review delay remains the fallback when he is waiting on someone else.

Existing projects: after updating in Installed Agents, a planning commitment Blocked by the earlier release is released once and reassessed. Estimates, capacity and sprint preflight still determine which published tasks may start.

Validation: 603 regressions, including the resolved-issue replay and an unrelated resource change after admission.
