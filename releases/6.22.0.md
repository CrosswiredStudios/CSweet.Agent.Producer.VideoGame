# 6.22.0

Changes how planning handles ownership findings and failures, so the team reaches its first Story without a manual restart. Requires Agent SDK 5.40.0.

## The scope-ownership review is advisory

- A gap found by the ownership review is opened as an issue on the board and sent to the Technical Director as before, but it no longer holds work. Gabriel keeps selecting, reviewing and publishing Stories while it is open.
- Every approved obligation must still be owned before final acceptance. An open gap prevents the project from being concluded.
- One kind of finding still holds work: an established conflict between two binding requirements, which needs management direction. It holds only the scope it affects and the work that depends on it, until the decision is recorded.
- If the ownership review itself fails, planning continues and the review is judged again on the next pass.
- The review no longer receives the roster or resource state and is told that staffing, capacity and readiness are never ownership gaps. Previously it reported a missing engineer while that hire was completing, which held the whole roadmap.

## Recoverable failures are retried

- When the model cannot produce an acceptable planning reply, Gabriel's commitment waits and retries after 1, 2, 5, 15 and 30 minutes, reading current facts each time. It is Blocked only if all retries fail, and then with the exact rejection. Previously this was reported as a technical issue needing a code correction and was never retried.
- Unexpected failures anywhere in Gabriel's personal work follow the same retry budget and keep their real cause.
- A rejected review reply now gets the specific correction, for example that `nextCandidate` is a zero-based index. Previously every rule returned the same combined message.
- When a binding conflict holds every remaining candidate and no sprint has finished, Gabriel waits on that decision instead of asking the model for a selection that cannot be valid.

## Existing projects

After updating, a planning commitment Blocked by an earlier release is released once and reassessed. Scope that was held by an ordinary gap becomes eligible. Holds that are awaiting a management decision stay in place until it is decided.

Validation: 609 regressions, including three scripted journeys from an accepted roadmap to a published Story that replay the November 15 to 17 stalls.
