# 3.0.1

Planning blockers now identify the unresolved questions, current decision IDs and outcomes,
recorded missing evidence, production follow-up owner and next actions. Resolved questions and
unrelated or superseded decision evidence are excluded. A new brief is requested only for an
actual accepted-scope change.

Routine reviews retain unchanged decision blockers instead of repeatedly requeueing the same
planning ticket. Authorized current-state reads recover changed decisions or planning scope on
decision events and bounded reconnect/attention discovery. Explicit retry and existing scope,
staffing, estimation and QA gates remain intact.

The current sprint policy still limits preparation to research spikes while an authority question
is unresolved; this release reports that limitation rather than silently widening authority.
Upgrade the installed Producer to apply the change; existing generic blockers are reassessed on
the next normal review. Historical comments are preserved.
