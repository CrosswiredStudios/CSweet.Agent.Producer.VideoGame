# 2.15.0

- The kickoff to the Producer's manager now names the next step for both kinds of manager: a Creative
  Director shares the accepted pitch and high-level GDD; an owner-manager can start from a short brief.
  When the manager is not the hiring owner, the owner is told who the Producer reports to instead of
  being asked for a goal.
- The Producer records what it is waiting for (`producer-handoff-watch`). While it has no visible project,
  attention reviews follow up with the manager after 30 minutes (twice for an agent manager, once for an
  owner-manager) and then tell the owner once in the onboarding conversation. Each follow-up DM is also a
  kickoff wake for the Creative Director, so it re-triggers the idempotent documentation handoff.
- Producers onboarded by an earlier version start the watch on their next review and follow up with
  their manager; they do not escalate because the owner conversation was not recorded.
- No new capabilities or grants.
