# 6.22.1

Routes the recoverable failures between an accepted brief and sprint start to retry-later, so they wait and try again instead of blocking or stalling silently. Requires Agent SDK 5.40.0.

## Technical Director failures

- When the Technical Director cannot generate a valid roadmap, Gabriel waits and retries after 1, 2, 5, 15 and 30 minutes, requesting a fresh generation each time. Previously the first failure was a permanent block, and asking again returned the same failed session.
- When the Technical Director fails to refine a selected Story, or returns a Story that does not pass validation, Gabriel sets it aside and asks again on the retry under a new request, telling him what was wrong. Previously this was a permanent block. A cancelled refinement still blocks.
- When a planning proposal comes back bound to different inputs, a fresh one is requested on the retry.

## Estimates and sprint preparation

- A failed estimate request is retried with a new request. Previously it was reported as "Role estimation is pending" and polled every 15 minutes indefinitely.
- A stale or incomplete estimate proposal is requested again on the retry instead of blocking.
- An unavailable roster, planning state that cannot be read, changed planning evidence and a conflicting sprint returned at creation are retried instead of blocking.

## Unchanged

These still block, because a person or authority has to act: missing commitment context, a planning conflict that needs the Video Game Director, unresolved authority questions, structural problems in the selected tickets, a missing Ready column, and an installed Technical Director without roadmap support. Each retry is bounded; when the budget is spent the commitment is Blocked with the exact failure.

Existing projects: after updating, a planning commitment Blocked by a Technical Director generation failure is released once and a new generation is requested. Update the Technical Director to 6.13.2 first.

Validation: 611 regressions, including a journey through the real planning commitment where a failed generation is retried with a fresh request, and a journey where a failed Story refinement is asked again with its reason.
