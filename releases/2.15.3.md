# 2.15.3

- When the platform refuses to hand a ticket to its assignee (for example, the assignee isn't a participant of the
  project), the ticket is now parked as Blocked with the reason, and the Producer raises it to his manager once with
  that reason and the `Retry ticket` / `Amend ticket` replies. Previously the sprint stalled silently. With the
  matching host change, staffing hires are enrolled in their team's project automatically, so this only fires when a
  person with authority over the project has to decide.
- No new capabilities or grants.
