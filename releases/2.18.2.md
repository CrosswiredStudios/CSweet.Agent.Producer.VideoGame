# 2.18.2

- When the platform denies the Producer access it needs to run delivery (for example `work.delivery.read.v1` on a project it manages but isn't a member of), the delivery-recovery commitment no longer crashes and blocks itself with "execution stopped unexpectedly". The Producer keeps the commitment waiting with the exact reason and rechecks every 20 minutes.
- The refusal is raised to the Producer's manager once per board and capability. The message names the refused capability and the platform's reason, says to add the Producer under Projects → Manage members, and follows the delivery-escalation convention so a Creative Director passes it to the CEO.
- New reply `Retry staffing: <what changed>` from the reporting chain makes the Producer recheck waiting or blocked delivery staffing immediately.
- Pairs with the C-Sweet platform change that enrolls a project's accountable manager, with its delivery access, when the manager creates the project board.
