# 6.23.0

The Producer retains the failed specialist session and retries the same Story under a stable attempt key. After upgrading from a version that lost the session pointer, the Producer uses board-scoped coordination discovery to recover the original request and validate the specialist’s result against its accepted source and outline. A recovered draft goes through the normal admission review with its artifact digest.

Requires Agent SDK 5.41.0, a host supporting board contribution discovery and the team-scoped coordination list capability.

Recovered work linked to a previous planning escalation requires a disposition from its issuing authority. New refinement requests expose a draft title on the board. After the Producer reviews the returned task briefs, a missing author capability can enter the governed staffing workflow during planning.
