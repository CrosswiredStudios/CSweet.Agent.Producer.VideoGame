# 6.0.4

Keep unfinished planned tickets visible when pending planning decisions exclude every sprint candidate. Readiness now records the affected ticket identifiers and current accepted-source decision evidence instead of falsely completing with no work remaining. Normal discovery creates a new readiness generation for old false completions, while unchanged decision fingerprints suppress repeated requeues and changed evidence permits recovery. Scope, estimates, independent QA readiness and sprint preflight remain mandatory; this release does not approve blocked implementation.
